## Database modernization for AI

I help teams get their databases ready to reliably support AI systems. Typical engagements include:

**Performance & indexing audits** — finding and fixing the queries, indexes, and schema decisions that are quietly throttling your application and any AI workloads built on top of it.

**CI/CD for data platforms** — bringing schema changes, migrations, and data pipeline deployments under the same discipline as application code, so changes are tested and reversible instead of risky.

**Vector database & AI semantics readiness** — evaluating and implementing vector search, embeddings storage, and retrieval patterns so your AI features are grounded in accurate, well-modeled data.

**Data engineering** — building reliable pipelines that get clean, well-structured data where it needs to be, on schedule.

**Modernization roadmaps** — for teams that know their database needs work but aren't sure where to start, a prioritized plan for getting from where you are to AI-ready.
